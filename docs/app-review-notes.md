Notes:
Sequins helps people find local drag shows, buy tickets, and tip performers. The demo account is set up as a performer and host:
- Discover tab: browse upcoming Portland shows. Some shows are public listings we added that are marked "Is this you? Claim this show" until the real host claims them.
- Tickets tab: the demo account holds a free ticket to "Friday Night Extravaganza," with its door QR code.
- Organize tab: the demo account hosts "Bea's Late Night Cabaret" (free show), with the door check-in screen.
- Sequins Pro (monthly subscription) and the 7-day Boost are sold through In-App Purchase (Profile > Subscription, and Boost on an event or performer profile).
- Event tickets, tips to performers, and payments to event staff are for live, in-person events and services, so they go through Stripe (Guideline 3.1.3(e)).
- "Contact host" sends an email through our relay; there is no in-app chat.
- Payout Setup opens Stripe's own onboarding for hosts and performers to receive money.

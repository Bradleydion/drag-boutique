## 5. Bookings, Tickets & Payments

All payments on Sequins — ticket purchases, commission sales, and tips — are processed through **Stripe**. By making or receiving a payment on Sequins, you also agree to the [Stripe Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/connect-account) and Stripe's own terms. Sequins is a facilitator of these transactions, not a party to the underlying sale between a buyer and a host, performer, or seller.

### Service fee

Sequins charges a service fee on **ticket sales** and **commission sales** (custom work booked through a performer's Commissions feature):

- The fee starts at **7%** of the sale price.
- The fee scales down the more a host or seller uses Sequins — measured by shows posted (for ticket sales) or commissions completed (for commission sales) — to a floor of **4%**.
- The exact volume thresholds for each tier are set by Sequins and may be updated from time to time; current thresholds are shown in your host/seller dashboard in the app.

**Tips are never subject to this fee.** 100% of any tip goes to the performer.

### Refunds and cancellations

Sequins facilitates the transaction between the buyer and the seller (host or performer) — refund decisions belong to the seller, within the structure below:

- When creating a ticketed event or a commission listing, a host or performer can set a **refund window** (e.g., "refunds accepted up to 7 days before the event" or "up to 24 hours after a commission is booked"). Buyers can request a refund through the app up until that window closes.
- Alternatively, a host or performer can mark a listing **"All Sales Are Final,"** in which case no refunds are offered through the app for that listing, and this is disclosed to the buyer before purchase.
- If an event is cancelled by the host, or a performer cancels a confirmed commission, Sequins will make a full refund request available to affected buyers regardless of the listing's stated refund window.
- Sequins does not independently arbitrate refund disputes beyond facilitating the request and the Stripe refund itself; the underlying obligation to deliver the event or commission is between the buyer and the seller.

*(Note: the in-app refund request and approval flow referenced above is a feature under active development and must be live before this policy is published.)*

- **Booking requests between hosts and performers** are agreements between those users. Sequins does not guarantee that a booked performer will appear, that an event will occur as listed, or the quality or safety of any event. Any dispute about a booking, cancellation, or no-show is between the parties involved.
